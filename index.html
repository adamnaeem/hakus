What the periodic inspection covers

Section 01 / Checklist

Katsastus is the mandatory roadworthiness check required by Finnish law for every registered car. A new car is inspected for the first time when it turns four, then regularly after that. The due date is printed on your registration certificate. Driving with an overdue inspection can lead to a fine and invalid insurance.

CheckpointWhat we look atMethodResult
Brakes Braking force, balance and handbrake on the roller tester Measured Pass / Fail
Lights & electrics Headlight aim, indicators, brake lights, horn, battery Beam test Pass / Fail
Emissions Exhaust gas analysis, checked against limits for your model Measured Pass / Fail
Suspension & steering Shock absorbers, joints, play in steering components Measured Pass / Fail
Body & rust Structural corrosion, sharp edges, doors, seatbelt mounts Visual Pass / Fail
Tyres & wheels Tread depth (min. 1.6 mm summer, 3 mm winter), damage Visual Pass / Fail

If your car fails: you have one month to repair the faults and return for a re-inspection. Because our mechanics work in the same building, most faults can be quoted and fixed on the spot, and the re-inspection is discounted when the repair is done with us.
